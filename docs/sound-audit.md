Footstep columns show the configured sound when the player can step on the block; collision geometry and supporting blocks can affect what is heard. Creative breaking can suppress break audio.

Overlays play generic brushing on application and cinnabar breaking on removal. Footsteps come from the supporting block. This is a static source audit, not an in-game listening test.
